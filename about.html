Dhara Prime Realty is a specialized commercial real estate firm based in Hyderabad, dedicated to corporate leasing, strategic property sales, and high-yield investment advisory.

Founded in 2025 by Rajashekar Sandyapaga, the firm was conceived to solve the fundamental frictions in commercial real estate through a transparent, data-driven and client-first approach.

By integrating hyper-local market intelligence across Hyderabad's western corridor, including HITEC City, Financial District, Gachibowli, Kokapet, and Bachupally, we enable clients to make informed property decisions with confidence.
